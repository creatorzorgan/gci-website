Marks a private body may not use

  • The State Emblem of India (Lion Capital) or "Satyameva Jayate": State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005.
  • The Ashoka Chakra, the national flag or a map of India as part of the mark.
  • Any foreign royal or state arms, such as the British royal coat of arms.
  • The ® symbol until the mark is registered (Trade Marks Act, 1999, s.107). Use ™ or nothing.

07 / Rules

How it behaves

The principles the portal is built on.

A

Navy, never black

Deep Obsidian Navy is the field everywhere. One luminous object sits on it: the galaxy.

B

Accents are rationed

Gold for authority, amber for action and status, crimson held in reserve. No per-section colour themes.

C

No invented telemetry

Numbers on screen are true ones only: 6 councils, 17 divisions, 72 initiatives, section index, scroll progress.

D

Design speaks first

Names and numbers on screen, charter text one tap away. Every word of the charter stays on the site.

E

Phones first

Every change is checked on small phones, then tablet and desktop. Nothing runs under the header or bottom bar.

F

Lawful marks only

No national emblem, flag, map of India or foreign arms, and no ® until the mark is registered.
