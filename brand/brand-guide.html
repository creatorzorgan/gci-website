05 / The form

One galaxy, eight states

One cloud of particles that reshapes itself. It begins as the galaxy and resolves into one form per council. Each is drawn from the same generated data the portal uses.

06 / Imagery

The galaxy, in use

Frames from the briefing portal. The galaxy is the only image the brand uses: edge-on beside the headline, opened out as the reader scrolls, split into six silos to show fragmentation, then resolved into each council's form.

07 / Rules

How it behaves

The principles the portal is built on.

A

One luminous object

The galaxy on a dark field. No stock imagery, no illustration, no second focal point.

B

Accents are rationed

Gold for authority, cyan for instruments, crimson once. No per-section colour themes.

C

No invented telemetry

Numbers on screen are true ones only: 6 councils, 33 programmes, section index, scroll progress.

D

Text holds still

Copy swaps in place in a fixed column. The form moves; the words do not travel.

E

Long holds, short moves

Scrolling mostly rotates the form. Large transitions are used three times in the whole page.

F

One thread

A thin gold strand of particles runs through every section, so the page reads as one journey.
